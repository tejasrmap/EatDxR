## Google Play Account Deletion Policy Compliance

In accordance with the **Google Play Developer Program Policy regarding Account Deletion**, Madeater allows all registered users to request the permanent and irreversible deletion of their account and all associated personal data.

### 1. What Data is Permanently Deleted?
Upon deletion, the following data is permanently purged:
- **Personal Identity Data:** Display Name, Critic Username, email address, profile picture, and bio.
- **Culinary Content:** All meal entries, reviews, scores, rankings, custom food lists, and itineraries.
- **Media Uploads:** All uploaded food photos and short-form video cravings (reels).
- **Taste DNA:** Flavor profiles, quiz answers, and AI recommendations.
- **Social Graph:** Following lists, followers, likes, comments, and notifications.

### 2. Data Retention Period
- **Immediate Action:** Account access and credentials are immediately deactivated.
- **Permanent Purge:** All database records in Firebase Firestore and cloud storage buckets in Supabase are permanently deleted within **7 business days**. No retained personal data is kept after completion.

## How to Delete Your Account

### Option 1: Web Request (No App Installation Required)
If you no longer have the Madeater app installed, you can submit an account deletion request via our public web portal:
👉 **[https://madeater.in/delete-account](https://madeater.in/delete-account)**

Or email developer support directly:
- **To:** [tejag.vijay@gmail.com](mailto:tejag.vijay@gmail.com)
- **Subject:** `Madeater Account Deletion Request`
- **Body:** State your registered email address and username.

---

### Option 2: Directly Inside the Madeater Mobile App
1. Open the **Madeater** app on your device.
2. Tap your **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation dock.
3. Tap the **Settings (Gear)** icon in the top right.
4. Scroll down to the **Privacy & Safety** section.
5. Tap **Delete Account & Data** and confirm your decision.
