## 1. Overview & Purpose
This Privacy Policy describes how **Madeater** ("we", "our", or "the App") collects, uses, stores, and protects user data. Madeater is a social platform and culinary diary designed for food critics and lovers to log meals, rate dishes, share video cravings, curate ranked food guides, and discover dining venues.

We strictly comply with the **Google Play Developer Program Policies**, including the **User Data Policy** and global data privacy regulations (such as GDPR, CCPA, and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act).

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Personal & Profile Information
- **Identity Details:** Display Name and unique Critic Username.
- **Contact Info:** Registered email address for authentication and security verification.
- **Profile Data:** Profile photo, biography, and flavor preferences (Taste DNA quiz).

### B. User-Generated Food Content
- **Dining Experiences:** Dish logs, ratings (0.5 to 10.0), and restaurant reviews.
- **Media Content:** Photos and short-form video cravings (reels) uploaded by users.
- **Curated Lists:** Custom guides, ranked lists, and food crawl itineraries.
- **Social Engagement:** Likes, comments, and following lists.

### C. Location & Food Radar
- **Location Data:** Approximate or precise location, requested strictly with explicit user consent.
- **Usage:** Used exclusively to discover restaurants nearby and populate the Food Radar discovery map. Continuous background location tracking is never enabled.

### D. Device & Technical Diagnostics
- **Device Details:** Hardware model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
- **Notification Tokens:** Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) token for push alerts.
- **Performance:** Crash analytics to maintain application stability and performance.

---

## 3. Device Permissions & Usage

Madeater requests permissions only when required by user-initiated features:

| Permission | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **`CAMERA`** | Used to capture dish photos or craving video clips directly inside the app. Never accessed in the background. |
| **`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` / `STORAGE`** | Used to upload dish photos and video clips from the device's photo gallery. |
| **`ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION` / `ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`** | Used to discover nearby dining venues and calculate proximity to restaurants. |
| **`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`** | Used to send notifications for social interactions (likes, comments, critic follows). |

---

## 4. Third-Party Service Providers

To ensure secure and reliable operation, Madeater integrates with industry-standard providers:
- **Google Firebase (Google LLC)**: Authentication, Firestore cloud database, media storage, push notifications, and analytics. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Supabase**: Cloud database synchronization and media storage buckets.
- **Google Maps Platform**: Geocoding, place lookup, and restaurant discovery.
- **OpenRouter / Gemini AI**: Processing AI Food Concierge recommendation queries (anonymized).

---

## 5. Data Security & Encryption

All network traffic between Madeater and cloud servers is encrypted in transit using **HTTPS (TLS 1.3)**. Authentication tokens are securely stored using modern encrypted storage to prevent unauthorized account access.

---

## 6. Account Deletion & Data Retention (Google Play Mandate)

Users have the unconditional right to delete their Madeater account and all associated food logs, reviews, ratings, photos, and personal data at any time:

1. **In-App Self Service:** Open Madeater > *Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account*.
2. **Direct Email Request:** Send an email to **[tejag.vijay@gmail.com](mailto:tejag.vijay@gmail.com)** with the subject *"Delete Madeater Account and Data"* from your registered email. Data deletion is permanently completed within 7 business days.
