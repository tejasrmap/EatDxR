Request Madeater Account & Data Deletion

Official account and personal data deletion instructions for the Madeater Mobile App (Package ID: com.madeater.app).

1. What Data Will Be Permanently Deleted?

When you request account deletion, all personal information and user-generated content associated with your identity is permanently wiped:

  • Profile Data: Name, critic username, email address, profile photo, and biography.
  • Culinary Records: All meal logs, ratings, dish reviews, and custom food lists.
  • Media Uploads: All uploaded dish photographs and short-form craving video reels.
  • Taste DNA: Flavor preference quiz answers and culinary recommendations.
  • Social Connections: Following lists, followers, likes, and comments.

Retention Period: Account deactivation occurs immediately. Complete database and storage purge is fulfilled within 7 business days.

2. How to Request Deletion via Web / Email

If you have uninstalled the app or prefer to request deletion online, send an email to our developer support:

To: tejag.vijay@gmail.com

Subject: Madeater Account Deletion Request

Body: Please include your registered email address and username.

Send Deletion Email

3. How to Delete Directly Inside the Madeater Mobile App

  1. Open the Madeater app.
  2. Tap your Profile icon on the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings (Gear) icon at the top right.
  4. Under Privacy & Safety, tap Delete Account & Data.
  5. Confirm your decision to instantly delete your account.
