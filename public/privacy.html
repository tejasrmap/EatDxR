Privacy Policy for ApecERP

This document details the policies regarding the collection, processing, usage, and safeguarding of data within the ApecERP Mobile Application.

Effective Date: September 2026 • App Package: com.apec.erp / ApecERP • Target: Students, Faculty & Enterprise Staff

1. Overview and Purpose

The ApecERP Mobile App is an institutional enterprise resource planning system created to streamline campus workflows, academic record management, digital attendance verification, and administrative communications.

We take user privacy with the utmost seriousness. This policy adheres strictly to Google Play Store policies (including User Data and Financial Data policies) and standard data protection regulations.

2. Types of Information Collected

We only collect data necessary for functional operations within your educational institution or enterprise:

A. Personal Identity & Academic Info

  • Full Name & Roll Number / Employee ID
  • Institutional Email Address & Phone Number
  • Department, Branch, Course, and Semester
  • Profile Picture (optional, uploaded by user)

B. Device & Diagnostic Data

  • Device model, manufacturer & Android version
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Token for alerts
  • IP address, network state & connectivity checks
  • System crash logs to diagnose software errors

3. Device Permissions & Purpose

The app requests the following Android runtime permissions solely for specific functional features:

android.permission.CAMERA

Used strictly to scan dynamic QR codes during class attendance or capture profile photos. The camera is never accessed in the background or for any unannounced purpose.

android.permission.READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / STORAGE

Used to allow students and faculty to upload assignments, syllabus documents, and download academic certificates/grade reports onto local storage.

android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to deliver real-time notifications regarding exam schedules, timetable adjustments, circular notices, and fee payment deadlines.

android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Optional)

If your institution implements geofence-based on-campus attendance verification, location is checked strictly once at the moment of check-in and is never continuously tracked.

4. Third-Party Integrations

The application connects with trusted standard infrastructure tools:

  • Google Play Services: For application distribution, security validation, and OS libraries.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM): For encrypted delivery of push notifications.
  • Firebase Crashlytics / Analytics: For crash reporting and bug resolution.

Third-party tools do not sell or monetize personal user records.

5. Security and Encryption

All communications between the mobile application and institutional servers are strictly protected with HTTPS and TLS 1.3 encryption in transit. Authentication credentials and tokens are secured via encrypted device storage (Android Keystore).

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

In accordance with Google Play's User Data & Account Deletion Policy, users have full rights to request the deletion of their mobile account and stored data:

How to Request Account & Data Deletion:

1. In-App Option: Navigate to Profile > Account Settings > Request Data Deletion.

2. Direct Email Request: Send an email from your registered email address to admin@apecerp.com or tejag.vijay@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request - [Your Roll/ID Number]". Account access and associated telemetry data will be removed within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

ApecERP is built for educational and enterprise environments. Any data involving minors under 18 is administered solely under institutional authority with consent from educational authorities or legal guardians.
