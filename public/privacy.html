Privacy Policy for Madeater

This Privacy Policy explains how Madeater collects, uses, protects, and discloses personal data for users of the Madeater Mobile Application.

Effective Date: September 2026 • App ID: com.madeater.app • Developer Contact: tejag.vijay@gmail.com

1. Overview & Scope

Madeater is a mobile and web application dedicated to food culture, culinary logging, restaurant ratings, and social food discovery (often referred to as "Letterboxd for Food").

We take user privacy seriously and adhere strictly to the Google Play Developer Distribution Agreement, User Data Policies, GDPR, and global data privacy standards. This policy provides full transparency regarding what data we handle and your rights.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide culinary logging, social interaction, and restaurant discovery services:

A. Account & Profile Data
  • Display Name and unique Critic Username
  • Email address (for authentication and security alerts)
  • Profile picture and biography
  • Flavor preferences and Taste DNA quiz results
B. Food Logs & Content
  • Dining entries, ratings (0.5 to 10.0), and written food reviews
  • Uploaded food photographs and short-form video cravings
  • Curated food lists and food itinerary trails
  • Social activity: likes, comments, and following lists
C. Location & Radar Data
  • Approximate and/or precise location (when explicitly granted)
  • Used solely to discover restaurants near you and populate the Food Radar map
  • No continuous background location tracking is ever conducted
D. Device & Diagnostics
  • Device model, OS version, and network connectivity status
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Anonymous crash logs to improve app reliability

3. Device Permissions & Usage

CAMERA

Used solely when you choose to take a photo or short video clip of a dish or meal to attach to your review. No background recording occurs.

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / STORAGE

Used to select existing dish photos or craving video clips from your local photo gallery to include in your meal logs.

ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION / ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION

Used to pinpoint your city or neighborhood for local restaurant discovery and distance calculation to dining venues.

POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to deliver alerts when someone comments on your review, likes your post, or when a critic you follow publishes a new craving.

4. Third-Party Services

Madeater integrates with verified service providers to ensure secure, high-performance operation:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): User authentication, Firestore cloud database, media storage, push notifications, and analytics.
  • Supabase: Cloud database synchronization and media storage buckets.
  • Google Maps Platform: Geocoding and venue discovery.
  • OpenRouter / Gemini AI: AI Food Concierge recommendation queries.

5. Data Security & Storage

All communications between the Madeater app and backend services are encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS (TLS 1.3). Authentication credentials are protected with modern, encrypted token storage.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention (Google Play Mandate)

In strict compliance with Google Play's Account Deletion Policy, users can permanently delete their account and all associated food reviews, ratings, photos, and personal information at any time:

  • In-App Self Service: Open Madeater > Profile > Settings > Account > Delete Account.
  • Email Request: Send an email to tejag.vijay@gmail.com with subject "Delete Madeater Account and Data" from your registered email address. All data will be permanently wiped within 7 business days.

7. Children's Privacy

Madeater does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe an underage user has provided information, please contact us immediately to have the data removed.
